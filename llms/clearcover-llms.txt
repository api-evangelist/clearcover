# Clearcover

> Clearcover is a digital-first auto insurance company offering customizable, affordable car
> insurance with AI-assisted claims that can resolve eligible claims in minutes. Policies are
> managed through a mobile app; the company also runs an independent-agent channel and a
> developer portal for partner/embedded integrations.
